Welcome! Discover our approach to white patches .

Welcome to the Unani Research Centre, associated with Vaidya Vinit Kumar. We understand that skin conditions can bring many questions and concerns. Our aim is to provide Unani medicines and a respectful space to discuss white patches (vitiligo/leucoderma), psoriasis and piles, and help people understand possible next steps.

Our centre provides Unani medicine for vitiligo, psoriasis and piles. Every person's needs are different, and an appropriate medical assessment can help determine the right course of action. We don't just give medicines, we create fully customised medicines according to the patient's skin type.
